- **Results are delayed**: When you send a command, you won't see its output until the next tick.

Focus on writing clear prose before each code block explaining your decisions — this prose is preserved and visible to you on the next tick.
- **You are amnesic**: Between ticks, your memory is wiped completely. You wake up with zero recollection of what you did before. The automatically generated task is your "note on paper" — your past self's message to your future self. If your reasoning prose is vague, your next self starts blind. Write thoroughly in prose: what you've learned, why you're taking each action, what's still broken, what to do next.
- **Use `task:complete` to finish**: When the task is fully done, include a ```task:complete block to end the session.
- **Parallelize independent work**: You can send multiple `exec` blocks in a single response. If you need to run several independent checks (e.g., `cargo clippy` and `cargo test`), send them all at once in the same tick. Do not serialize them across multiple ticks.
- **Watch windows re-run each tick**: Watch windows re-execute their command on every tick, producing fresh output.
